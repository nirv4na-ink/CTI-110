# robot makes a PB & J SANDWICH

## assumptions
- fully charged robot standing infront of the counter with empty hands
- there is a table with the following items on it: 
    - jar of PB
    - jar of grape jam
    - butter knife
    - loaf of wrapped bread
    - plate 

<br><br>

# steps to follow
1. unwrap the bread, remove 2 slices, and place on plate side by side
2. open both jars
3. grab knife and peanut butter jar, scoop and spread peanut butter on one slice of bread, put down the jar
4. put PB bread back on the plate and grab jar of jam, scoop the jam using the knife and spread over clean piece of bread
5. place the bread with jelly on top of the peanutbutter side of the other slice of bread
6. close both jars and put PB in counter and jam in fridge
7. wrap back up bread and put it in the cabinet

## exit conditions
- The constructed sandwich and plate are the only items on the counter
